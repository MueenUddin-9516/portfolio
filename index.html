I study how to keep intelligent, connected systems trustworthy when attackers, scale and new computing models all push the other way.

My work spans AI and agentic-AI security, blockchain and decentralised identity, post-quantum and lightweight cryptography, federated learning, IoT and cyber-physical systems security, digital forensics and privacy-enhancing technologies. Across an 18-year career in Qatar, Malaysia, Saudi Arabia, Brunei, the UAE, Oman and Pakistan, that has produced more than 220 peer-reviewed papers, 180+ of them in Q-ranked journals.

Alongside research, I lead and design cybersecurity courses, supervise PhD and master's research, and serve as an associate editor for IEEE, Springer Nature and other journals. I also contribute to the IEEE P3722 guide on responsible AI in higher education.
